# Aetheria AI — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 15, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 15, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Souta Aizawa Studio operates Aetheria AI, a Discord bot that provides AI-powered assistance.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, processed, transmitted, and retained when you interact with Aetheria AI.

The Bot provides features such as answering questions, summarizing text, and translating text.

In this Policy, "Aetheria AI," "the Bot," "we," "us," and "our" refer to the Bot and its operator, Souta Aizawa Studio, as applicable.

By using the Bot, you acknowledge that information may be processed as described in this Policy.

Please do not use the Bot to submit sensitive or confidential information that you do not want processed by the Bot or the third-party services it uses.

## 2. Information That May Be Processed

The information processed depends on how you interact with the Bot and how the relevant services operate.

### 2.1 Information You Submit

When you use an AI command, the Bot processes the text you provide.

Examples include:

- Questions submitted through `/ask`.
- Text submitted through `/summarize`.
- Text submitted through `/translate`.
- A target language supplied for a translation.
- Other information included in a Discord command.

The submitted text is used to perform the requested operation.

Please avoid submitting passwords, authentication tokens, payment credentials, sensitive personal information, or confidential material.

### 2.2 Discord Interaction Information

When you interact with the Bot, Discord provides information needed to process the interaction.

Depending on the command and implementation, this may include:

- Your Discord user ID.
- The Discord server or guild ID.
- The Discord channel ID.
- The command you used.
- The text and options submitted with the command.
- Interaction metadata needed to respond to the command.

The Bot may receive this information as part of the Discord interaction even if it does not save that information in a database.

### 2.3 Technical and Operational Information

The Bot's application and hosting environment may generate technical information, such as:

- Error messages.
- Diagnostic information.
- Application startup and shutdown messages.
- Service availability information.
- Other operational logs generated by the application or hosting provider.

The application code reviewed for this Policy contains error logging, but does not explicitly log users' complete prompts or conversations.

The hosting provider may independently generate logs or process technical information. The exact information retained depends on the hosting service and its configuration.

### 2.4 Information from Third-Party Services

Discord, OpenAI, and the Bot's hosting provider may process information under their own terms, privacy policies, and configurations.

We do not control every type of information these services independently collect or retain.

Please review the relevant third-party privacy policies for additional details.

## 3. How the Bot Uses Information

Information processed by Aetheria AI may be used to:

- Answer questions submitted through `/ask`.
- Summarize text submitted through `/summarize`.
- Translate text submitted through `/translate`.
- Process Discord interactions.
- Return responses to users.
- Maintain and operate the Bot.
- Diagnose errors and technical problems.
- Protect the service against abuse and security issues.
- Address legitimate operational or legal requirements.

We aim to limit information processing to purposes reasonably associated with providing, maintaining, and protecting the service.

## 4. AI Processing Through OpenAI

Aetheria AI uses OpenAI services to generate AI responses.

When you use an AI feature, the relevant submitted text is sent to OpenAI for processing.

For example:

- A question submitted through `/ask` may be sent to OpenAI to generate an answer.
- Text submitted through `/summarize` may be sent to OpenAI to produce a summary.
- Text submitted through `/translate` may be sent to OpenAI to produce a translation.

The information sent depends on the command and the Bot's implementation.

OpenAI processes information under its own applicable terms, policies, and service settings.

OpenAI Privacy Policy:

https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/

We do not control OpenAI's independent systems, retention practices, or processing decisions.

We do not guarantee that information sent to OpenAI is immediately deleted after a response is generated.

The handling and retention of information by OpenAI may depend on the service used, account configuration, applicable policies, and other relevant settings.

Users should avoid submitting information they do not want processed by an external AI service.

## 5. Data Storage and Conversation History

As of the date of this Policy, the application code reviewed for Aetheria AI does not implement a database-backed conversation history or persistent user-memory feature.

The reviewed code also does not explicitly log users' complete prompts or conversations.

However, the Bot processes information while handling Discord interactions and making AI requests.

Information may be processed or retained by Discord, OpenAI, the hosting provider, or other infrastructure under their respective policies and configurations.

We therefore do not promise that all information is deleted immediately after each interaction.

We also do not promise that third-party services retain no information.

Information stored directly by the Bot operator should be limited to what is reasonably necessary for legitimate operational purposes and applicable legal requirements.

If the Bot's implementation changes to introduce persistent conversation storage, user memory, analytics, or other data collection, this Policy should be updated to reflect those changes.

## 6. Discord Response Visibility

Aetheria AI uses Discord interactions to receive commands and return responses.

In the reviewed implementation, AI replies are not configured as ephemeral responses.

Depending on the interaction and channel permissions, a response may be visible to other people who can access that channel.

You should consider the channel's visibility and permissions before submitting information.

Do not assume that a response from the Bot is private merely because you submitted a slash command.

Discord independently processes interactions and content under its own policies.

Discord Privacy Policy:

https://discord.com/privacy
