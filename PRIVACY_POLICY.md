# Aetheria AI — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 15, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 15, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Aetheria AI is a Discord bot operated by Souta Aizawa Studio.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you interact with Aetheria AI through Discord.

The Bot provides AI-powered features, including answering questions, summarizing text, and translating text.

We aim to handle information responsibly and limit its use to purposes associated with operating, maintaining, and improving the service.

## 2. Information That May Be Processed

Depending on how you interact with the Bot, the following information may be processed.

### A. Content You Submit

When you use an AI feature, the text you submit may be processed to generate a response.

Examples include:

- Questions submitted to the Bot.
- Text submitted for summarization.
- Text submitted for translation.
- Other text included in your commands.

Please do not submit passwords, authentication tokens, payment information, or other sensitive or confidential information.

### B. Discord Information

Discord may provide the Bot with information needed to process your interactions. Depending on the interaction and implementation, this may include:

- Discord user ID.
- Discord server or guild ID.
- Discord channel ID.
- Command options and submitted text.
- Technical information associated with the interaction.

The information available to the Bot depends on its code, permissions, and configuration.

### C. Technical Information

The Bot's application and hosting environment may generate technical information, including error messages, timestamps, and diagnostic logs.

Whether these logs contain user IDs or submitted content depends on the implementation and hosting configuration.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information processed by Aetheria AI may be used to:

- Answer questions.
- Summarize submitted text.
- Translate text.
- Process Discord commands.
- Operate and maintain the Bot.
- Diagnose technical errors.
- Protect the Bot against abuse and security issues.

We aim to limit information processing to what is reasonably necessary for these purposes.

## 4. AI Processing and OpenAI

Aetheria AI uses OpenAI services to process AI requests.

When you use an AI feature, relevant text may be transmitted to OpenAI to generate a response.

The information sent depends on the feature used and the Bot's implementation.

OpenAI processes information according to its applicable terms, policies, and service settings.

OpenAI Privacy Policy:
https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/

We do not guarantee that information sent to OpenAI is immediately deleted after a response is generated. Its retention and handling may depend on the applicable service, account configuration, and policies.

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

The Bot's documented functionality does not describe a persistent conversation-history database.

However, information may still be processed or retained by Discord, OpenAI, the Bot's hosting provider, or application logs, depending on their respective configurations and policies.

We do not claim that all information is automatically deleted immediately after each interaction.

Information controlled by the Bot operator should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

## 6. How Information May Be Shared

Information may be processed by third-party services necessary to operate the Bot, including:

- **Discord:** Provides the platform through which users interact with the Bot.
- **OpenAI:** Processes AI requests.
- **Hosting provider:** Runs the Bot and may process technical or operational logs.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or operation of the Bot and its users.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy practices and applicable policies.

## 7. Sale of Personal Information

Souta Aizawa Studio does not intend to sell users' personal information.

This statement does not mean that third-party service providers never process information as part of providing their services.

## 8. Data Security

We aim to take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the Bot.

These measures may include protecting API keys, restricting access to deployment credentials, and avoiding unnecessary collection or logging of user content.

However, no online service or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should avoid submitting sensitive or confidential information to the Bot.
